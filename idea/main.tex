\begin{abstract}
We present a closed-loop simulation framework for vision-based autonomous
rendezvous with a tumbling, non-cooperative target, in which perception,
estimation and guidance are causally coupled: the camera viewpoint at each
step is a consequence of the pose solution at the previous one. The framework
combines Hill--Clohessy--Wiltshire relative dynamics, torque-free target
attitude propagation, a monocular keypoint front end, RANSAC--EPnP pose
estimation with manifold refinement, a multiplicative extended Kalman filter,
and LQR and receding-horizon controllers. Each subsystem is validated in
isolation --- the analytical state transition agrees with DOP853 integration
to $4.55\times10^{-10}$~m over three orbits, and RANSAC--EPnP solves every
trial up to 30\,\% outlier contamination where bare EPnP fails in 99\,\% of
trials at 5\,\%.
The closed-loop study identifies measurement \emph{availability}, not pose
accuracy, as the binding constraint. Pooled over 3887 control steps of 100
dispersed Monte Carlo trials, the pose solve succeeds on 100\,\% of steps
beyond 6~m and 1.8\,\% of steps inside 1~m; the transition is predicted in
closed form by the field-of-view fill range $z_{fill} = fL/W = 6.25$~m. In a
paired noise ablation the median terminal range is unchanged from 0.3 to
3.0~px while the pose error grows tenfold, and the degradation that follows
appears in the tail of the distribution --- the 95th percentile rises from
0.73~m to 12.47~m and docking success falls from 100\,\% to
85\,\% [71,\,93] --- rather than in the median. The design margin that matters
is therefore field of view and keypoint redundancy at short range, not
sub-pixel feature localisation.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and Code Availability}

The simulation framework, the experiment scripts that generate every figure
and table in this paper, and the per-trial Monte Carlo records are available
from the author on reasonable request. All results are reproducible from the
released code: each Monte Carlo trial is seeded from a single spawned seed
sequence and is therefore reproducible in isolation, independent of the
campaign size, of execution order, and of whether the campaign is run serially
or in parallel.

\section*{Use of Generative AI}

manuscript editing. All algorithms, experiments and results were specified,
executed and verified by the author, who takes full responsibility for the
content of this work.
